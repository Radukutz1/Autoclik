About AutoClik

AutoClik was born out of a simple necessity: the need for a fast, reliable, and strictly private automation tool that stays out of your way.

Developed by Image Cut , AutoClik follows the same core philosophy as our other web utilities like image-cut.com—focusing entirely on providing clean, zero-dependency tools that perform their tasks entirely locally, without tracking your data or compromising your security.

Whether you're using the Windows desktop client for heavy-duty tasks or the lightweight browser extension for quick automation, the goal remains the same: power up your workflow while respecting your system's resources.
